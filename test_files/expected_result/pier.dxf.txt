Mooring notes
Mooring lines are checked at every change of the tide, and doubled when the wind is forecast above force 6. Fenders hang between every pair of bollards; replace any that are split. Keep 1/2 a fathom of slack on the bow lines at high water.
No vessel may lie at the berth overnight without the leave of the harbour master. Depth	4 m at the berth head, drawing P4\B.
