Restoring the North Point Light
A proposal to the harbour trust, spring meeting

Why the light matters
The tower has guided ships past the reef since 1868
Fishing boats still steer by it on foggy nights
Visitors climb the stairs every summer weekend

What has worn out
Salt has eaten through the gallery railing
The lantern glass is cracked in four panes
Damp has rotted the floor of the keeper's room

The plan
Replace the railing with bronze in the first year
Order new glass from the works that made the old lens
Dry out and reopen the keeper's room as a small museum

Cost and timing
Railing and glass come to about two thirds of the budget
The museum fit-out is the remaining third
Work can begin in April and finish before the autumn storms
